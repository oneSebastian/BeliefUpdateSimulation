Welcome
We are researchers at the Interdisciplinary Transformation University Austria (IT:U).

About the Experiment:
The purpose of this study is to investigate the behavior of humans and LLM agents. You will be presented with a discussion topic and provided with a curated information packet on it. We ask you to engage with the information and afterwards whether that information changed your stance on the discussion topic. If applicable, give reasons for changing your stance. During the study you will not interact with LLMs in any way. However, we will ask you for some basic demographic information that will be used in a later study in setting up a comparable experiment with LLM agents that are instructed to follow the same demographic distribution as the participants of this study. We would appreciate your honesty in stating your opinions during the study. The experiment will approximately take 25 minutes. You will receive a flat base payment for participating in this study.

Privacy and Data Storage:
All data collected in this study will be anonymized. Even for the researchers, it will be impossible to identify you from the data we save. Your data will be kept confidential and saved under an anonymous ID that does not reveal your identity.

Usage in Published Material:
When the research is completed, the aggregate results of this study will be submitted for publication to a scientific journal. After publication, the data files will be uploaded to a public repository, allowing other scientists to independently verify the results. These publicized data files will not contain any information about the identity of the participants.

Participation and Withdrawal:
Your participation in this study is completely voluntary. At any time, you may refuse to participate without penalty or loss of benefits to which you may otherwise be entitled.

Please note:
Once you proceed with this survey, you will not be able to go back to previous questions or change your responses. We kindly ask that you read each question carefully and answer thoughtfully before submitting your response.
