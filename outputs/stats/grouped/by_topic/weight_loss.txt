------------------------------------------------------------------------------
READING THIS SUBSET -- topic = weight_loss
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
n = 391 here against 1173 for the whole design, so every
test is correspondingly less sensitive. Section 6 re-solves the minimum
detectable effect at this n -- compare each observed effect against it
before reading a non-significant result as an absence of a difference.

Paired d_z below 0.1768 is not resolvable at this n.

Section 4 compares the spread of only 9 comment mean-ranks per group
(the 27 comments are 3 topics x 3 packages x 3 messages, so a subset holds
a third or a ninth of them). A Brown-Forsythe test on groups that small has
almost no power; the SD and variance ratios remain descriptive, the p-values
essentially uninformative.
