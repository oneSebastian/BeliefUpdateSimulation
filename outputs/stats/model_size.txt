total/active are billions of parameters; they differ for MoE models and for Gemma's
effective-parameter (E) names. The table sorts on active -- compute per token.
Delta = mean(human - model) final stance; > 0 means the humans end more pro-topic.
p is the permutation test's own two-sided value, uncorrected. It tests Delta, the
paired SIGNED difference, so it goes with the Delta CI; the MAE CI is an interval for a
different quantity. p_holm is p times its Holm rank factor, capped at 1, across the
14 sources at alpha = 0.05 -- a different number, not a restatement of p.
Each CI is a participant-level bootstrap percentile interval. Its width comes from
variation between the 389 participants, not from the 10000 replicates: more
replicates pin the endpoints down more precisely, they do not narrow the interval.
valid% is the cases a model parsed and tries% the cases that used every retry allowed;
both are over ALL of a model's rows, before the complete-case intersection. Every score
above is computed on the intersection, so a low validity rate means that model's numbers
rest on the subset it could answer -- which is not a random subset.
